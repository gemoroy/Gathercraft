## v0.1.22

### Fixed
- **Sharing, too many answers to one question:** when somebody asked who knows more nodes of a zone, six or seven players answered where three were meant to. The lock only counted answers that had arrived before a player's own wait was over, and an answer that had started was sent to its end. Now every packet of an answer asks again, and the answer stops when three others were first. Who was first is told by the order of the channel, which is the same for everyone, so no two players stop each other. The wait before an answer is 2–15 s instead of 2–8 s. A harvest somebody shares in the meantime no longer counts as an answer. This is what a player on 0.1.22 does; older versions answer as before.
- **Sharing, answers that added nothing:** an answer was sent whenever the answering player knew more nodes than the asker – also when only 150 of them are sent and the asker knew 151. Now what is really sent (at most 150) must be at least a fifth and at least 20 nodes more than the asker knows. For an asker who knows fewer than 20 it is enough to know as many more as he knows; who knows nothing is told whatever there is. The price: who knows more than 125 new nodes of a zone gets no answer to a question any more. Harvests still reach him live.
- **Sharing, one more chat line after turning it off:** a line "… spots shared by other players added" that was already on its way still came after you unticked the option. It is dropped now.

### Added
- **`/gcr perf`:** one line about the last full minute – sync messages in and out, nodes read, what was refused (the budget per second, the limit per player), messages of other addons that pass by, the time spent in the sync code, and Gathercraft's memory where the client tells it. It only reads. The line is English in every language: it is meant to be sent to us.
- **Paths, collected for later:** Gathercraft notes which ways you ride. A zone is cut into squares of 25 yards; each crossing into a neighbouring square is counted with how hard it was (the way you made against the straight line), whether you fell or swam there, and the day. Only on this computer: nothing is sent, and no names, no times of day and no order of the squares are kept. Nothing uses the data yet – routes will, to go around obstacles. Not noted: instances, flight paths, while dead, in a vehicle, when you are carried or teleported. At most 30,000 path pieces (about 780 KB in the saved data); the ones not used for the longest go first. It is on by default: Options › Sharing › "Record the paths I ride". `/gcr paths` shows what is kept, `/gcr paths clear` deletes it and nothing else (it asks first). The client tells no height, so none is kept.

### Measured
- In our simulation of sharing (not the live game): one question to 40 players who know more is answered by 3 instead of 5, with 33 messages instead of 55. A whole realm in one zone (800 players, 5 minutes) sends 643 channel messages instead of 801, and the players know as many of the new nodes as before (98 % on average, was 96 %). On a normal evening (45 players) they know 93 % instead of 97 %: with exactly three answers a stranger's node is taken at once only when all three name it; the rest waits for its next confirmation.
