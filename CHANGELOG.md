## v0.1.22

### Fixed
- **Sharing, too many answers to one question:** when somebody asked who knows more nodes of a zone, six or seven players answered where three were meant to. The lock only counted answers that had arrived before a player's own wait was over, and an answer that had started was sent to its end. Now long answers come from at most three players; very short answers can still come from a few more. In detail: an answer of more than two messages is sent in full by the first three in the channel; everybody else stops after the first message of his answer at the latest, and nobody gives up for good before three others are seen to go on past their first message – so when somebody knows enough, at least one answer is sent in full. If a player's own messages do not come back to him, or come back late – we do not know yet what the realm does – the three whose names come first go on, and the others stop after their second message. If only some players get their own messages back in time, up to six can answer in full: three in the order of the channel and three by name. An answer of one or two messages cannot be held back when several players start it at the same moment: it is out before they hear each other. The wait before an answer is 2–15 s instead of 2–8 s. This is what a player on 0.1.22 does. Older versions keep their old rule.
- **Sharing, answers that added nothing:** an answer was sent whenever the answering player knew more nodes than the asker – also when only 150 of them are sent and the asker knew 151. Now what is really sent (at most 150) must be at least a fifth and at least 20 nodes more than the asker knows. For an asker who knows fewer than 20 it is enough to know as many more as he knows, but never fewer than two: who could add a single node does not answer, that node goes out with its next harvest. This has two prices. With exactly three answers, a stranger's node is taken at once only when all three name it; the others wait for further reports, which may or may not come. And who knows more than 125 new nodes of a zone gets no answer to a question any more: harvests still reach him live, but nothing brings him what he missed.
- **Sharing, one more chat line after turning it off:** a line "… spots shared by other players added" that was already on its way still came after you unticked the option. It is thrown away now the moment you untick it – also when you tick it again right away.

- **An error message in a fight:** with "Mark the node you walk towards" on, switching the questing mode in combat made the game show "Gathercraft tried to call the protected function 'SetCVar()'": the game lets no addon change a setting during a fight, and Gathercraft tried – for the mark, and the same way for the bobber settings and the louder splash. Now nothing is set or put back in combat. What is wanted at the end of the fight is done then, once: the mark comes then, and your own settings go back then. Until then the node or the bobber is not marked. The mistake was in the addon since 0.1.19.
- **German client: the skill plan now finds your Alchemy skill.** The game calls it "Alchimie" in the skill list and the profession window, and Gathercraft did not know that name: the skill plan kept asking you to open the profession window, and the recipes seen there were not filed under Alchemy. `/gcr crafttest` now also lists which profession each line of your skill list is read as, and with `/gcr debug` a line that looks like a profession but is none Gathercraft knows is named once per session.

### Added
- **`/gcr perf`:** one line about the last full minute – sync messages received, send attempts (not what arrived), nodes announced in the messages (before any check), what was refused (the budget per second, the limit per player), messages of other addons that pass by, the time in the handlers that receive and send, and Gathercraft's memory where the client tells it. It also says how many of your own messages came back to you since login. It only reads. The line is English in every language: it is meant to be sent to us.
- **Paths, collected for later:** Gathercraft notes which ways you ride. A zone is cut into squares of 25 yards; each crossing into a neighbouring square is counted with how hard it was (the way you made against the straight line), whether you fell or swam there, and the day. Only on this computer: nothing is sent, and no names, no times of day and no list of the places in the order you went are kept. Nothing uses the data yet – routes will, to go around obstacles. Not noted: instances, flight paths, while dead, in a vehicle, when you are teleported, and a crossing you are carried over or seen at with no speed of your own. At most 30,000 path pieces – about 780 KB in the saved data when all of them are there (an estimate from a full set in our test, not a fixed size); the ones not used for the longest go first. Saved path data that is damaged is put right when it is loaded. It is on by default: Options › Sharing › "Record the paths I ride". `/gcr paths` shows what is kept, `/gcr paths clear` deletes it and nothing else (it asks first). Gathercraft uses no height: where we looked, the game gave none.

### Measured
- In our simulation of sharing (not the live game), with the same players doing the same things before and after: one question to 40 players who know more is answered by 3 instead of 6, with 35 messages instead of 68; the asker, a stranger to all of them, has 70 of the 120 nodes at once instead of 119. A whole realm in one zone (800 players, 5 minutes) sends 654 channel messages instead of 783 and knows more of the new nodes (99 % on average, was 94 %); guild messages rise from 1,372 to 1,933, because answers in a full zone now get through. On a normal evening (45 players, 30 minutes) it is 229 messages instead of 259, and the players know 95 % of the new nodes instead of 98 %; who joins late knows 88 % after five minutes instead of 93 %. On a launch day (250 players) hardly anything changes: 428 messages instead of 436, 99 % known as before.
